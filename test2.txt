Artificial Intelligence (AI) is transforming industries across the world. 
It enables machines to learn from data, identify patterns, and make decisions 
with minimal human intervention.

Machine Learning is a subset of AI that focuses on training models using data.
Deep Learning is a specialized area that uses neural networks.

Applications of AI include:
- Healthcare (disease prediction)
- Finance (fraud detection)
- Transportation (self-driving cars)
- E-commerce (recommendation systems)

AI systems rely heavily on data quality. Poor data leads to poor predictions.
