CONSTRUCTION SITE SAFETY MANUAL

SECTION 1: HARDHAT REQUIREMENTS
All workers must wear an approved hardhat at all times when on an active construction site.
Hardhats protect against falling objects, bumps, and electrical hazards.
Workers found without a hardhat will be immediately removed from the site and issued a formal warning.
Repeated violations of the hardhat policy will result in suspension or termination.
Hardhats must meet ANSI/ISEA Z89.1 standards and be inspected daily for cracks or damage.

SECTION 2: SAFETY VEST REQUIREMENTS
High-visibility safety vests must be worn by all personnel in active work zones.
Safety vests must be Class 2 or Class 3 depending on the risk level of the work area.
Workers operating near moving vehicles or machinery must always wear a safety vest.
Failure to wear a safety vest in designated areas is a serious safety violation.
Safety vests must be kept clean and reflective strips must be clearly visible at all times.

SECTION 3: GLOVES REQUIREMENTS
Protective gloves must be worn when handling sharp materials, chemicals, or heavy equipment.
The correct type of glove must be selected based on the specific hazard present.
Torn or damaged gloves must be replaced immediately and must not be used.
Workers handling electrical equipment must use insulated gloves rated for the voltage level.

SECTION 4: GOGGLES AND EYE PROTECTION
Safety goggles or face shields must be worn when working with chemicals, power tools, or flying debris.
Standard prescription glasses do not qualify as safety eyewear.
Eye protection must meet ANSI Z87.1 standards.
Workers who experience eye irritation must report immediately to the site safety officer.

SECTION 5: FACE MASK REQUIREMENTS
Dust masks or respirators must be worn in areas with airborne particles, dust, or chemical fumes.
The correct mask type must be selected based on the specific hazard level.
N95 masks are required in areas with fine dust or biological hazards.
Masks must be properly fitted and inspected before each use.

SECTION 6: FALL DETECTION AND PREVENTION
Falls are the leading cause of serious injury on construction sites.
All workers must be secured with a harness when working at heights above 1.8 meters.
If a fall is detected or witnessed, immediately call emergency services at the site emergency number.
The area around the fall must be secured and no equipment moved until safety officers arrive.
All fall incidents must be reported within one hour using the incident report form.
Workers involved in a fall must receive medical evaluation before returning to work.

SECTION 7: LADDER SAFETY
Ladders must be inspected before every use for damage, loose rungs, or defects.
Only one person is permitted on a ladder at a time unless the ladder is specifically designed for multiple users.
Ladders must be positioned at the correct angle: one unit out for every four units of height.
Workers must maintain three points of contact with the ladder at all times.
Ladders must be secured at the top and bottom before climbing.

SECTION 8: SAFETY CONE AND HAZARD MARKING
Safety cones and hazard markers must be placed around all active work zones.
Cones must be positioned at least 2 meters from the edge of any hazard.
Workers must not remove or relocate safety cones without authorization from a site supervisor.
Inadequate hazard marking is a major safety violation subject to disciplinary action.

SECTION 9: ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES
First violation: Verbal warning and mandatory re-training session.
Second violation: Written warning placed in personnel file.
Third violation: Suspension from site for a minimum of 3 working days.
Fourth violation: Termination of contract and removal from site.
Serious violations including falls from height or injury to others may result in immediate termination.

SECTION 10: EMERGENCY PROCEDURES
Site emergency number: Call site supervisor immediately.
In case of serious injury, call national emergency services (911 or local equivalent).
First aid kits are located at the site office, main entrance, and each work zone.
All workers must complete emergency evacuation training during site induction.
